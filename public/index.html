About Resume Fixer

  

    Most resumes are read by an Applicant Tracking System (ATS) before a human ever sees them.
    These systems scan for specific keywords and phrases from the job posting — and if your resume
    is missing the exact terms it's looking for, it can get filtered out even if you're qualified.
  

  

    With Resume Fixer, you paste your resume and the job description, and it identifies the important
    keywords the ATS is scanning for that your resume is missing or underusing. It then rewrites
    your resume to naturally include them, using stronger action verbs and clean, ATS-friendly
    formatting — without inventing experience, employers, titles, or numbers that aren't true.
