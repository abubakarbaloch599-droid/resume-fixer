Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 10, 2026

1. What we collect

When you use Resume Fixer, we temporarily process the resume text and job description you submit in order to generate a rewritten resume. We do not require an account, name, or email to use the free or paid tiers.

2. How we use it

Your resume and job description text is sent to Anthropic's Claude API to generate the rewritten output. We do not store your resume content after your session ends, and we do not use your submitted content to train any AI models.

3. Payment information

Payments are processed by Paddle.com. We do not collect or store your card details — Paddle handles all payment information directly under their own privacy policy.

4. Cookies and local storage

We use your browser's local storage to track how many free fixes you have remaining. This data stays on your device and is not sent to us.

5. Third parties

We share data only with the service providers necessary to operate this tool: Anthropic (for AI processing) and Paddle (for payments).

6. Your rights

Since we do not store personal data or accounts, there is no stored personal data to request access to or delete. If you have concerns, contact us at abubakarbaloch599@gmail.com.

7. Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to abubakarbaloch599@gmail.com.
